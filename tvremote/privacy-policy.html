Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 9, 2026

TvRemote ("we", "our", "the app") is published by Dhamo. This policy explains how TvRemote handles your data.

1. Data We Collect

Local-only data (never leaves your phone):

  • TV names, IP addresses, and MAC addresses discovered on your local WiFi network
  • Pairing certificates stored in your device's app-private storage
  • User preferences (theme, last device, onboarding state)

Analytics data (sent to Google):

  • App usage events (app opened, device connected, feature tapped) via Firebase Analytics
  • Crash reports and stack traces via Firebase Crashlytics
  • Advertising identifiers via Google AdMob (free tier only)

2. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect your name, email, phone number, or any personal information
  • We do not access your contacts, photos, files, or location
  • We do not record or transmit any voice data — voice commands are processed locally on your device
  • We do not store any TV content or viewing history

3. How We Use Data

  • Firebase Analytics: To understand which features are used, improve the app experience, and diagnose issues
  • Crashlytics: To detect and fix crashes automatically
  • AdMob: To display non-intrusive banner ads to free-tier users. Pro subscribers see no ads.
  • Gemini AI: When you use the AI assistant (Pro feature), your text commands are sent to Google's Gemini API for processing. No conversation history is stored on our servers.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your data with any third party, except:

  • Google (Firebase, AdMob, Gemini): As described above, for analytics, crash reporting, advertising, and AI processing. Google's privacy policies apply to their services.
  • Google Play Billing: Subscription purchases are processed by Google Play. We do not have access to your payment information.

5. Data Storage & Security

  • All local data is stored in Android's app-private internal storage, encrypted at rest by the OS
  • TV communication uses TLS encryption (same protocol as Google's own remote app)
  • Pairing certificates are stored securely using Android Keystore-backed encryption
  • All network communication with Google services is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS)

6. Data Retention

  • Local data is retained until you uninstall the app or clear app data
  • Firebase Analytics data is retained for 14 months (Google default)
  • Crashlytics data is retained for 90 days

7. Your Rights

  • Delete your data: Clear app data or uninstall the app to remove all local data. For Firebase data, contact us at the email below.
  • Opt out of ads: Upgrade to Pro to remove all advertising
  • Opt out of analytics: Disable "Usage & diagnostics" in your Android device settings

8. Children's Privacy

TvRemote is rated for all ages and does not knowingly collect data from children under 13. The app does not require account creation or personal information to function.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
