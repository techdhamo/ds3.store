Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 11, 2026

DS3 Studio ("we", "our", "the company") develops privacy-first software products. This policy explains how our applications handle your data.

1. Data We Collect

Local-only data (never leaves your device):

  • Locally discovered network devices (e.g., TV names, IP addresses, MAC addresses)
  • Security certificates stored in your device's app-private storage
  • User preferences and settings (themes, layouts, onboarding state)

Analytics data (sent to Google):

  • App usage events (app opened, feature tapped) via Firebase Analytics
  • Crash reports and stack traces via Firebase Crashlytics
  • Advertising identifiers via Google AdMob (free tier users only)

2. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect your name, email, phone number, or any personal information
  • We do not access your contacts, photos, personal files, or location history
  • We do not record or transmit any voice data — voice commands are processed locally on your device
  • We do not store any viewing history or content logs

3. How We Use Data

  • Firebase Analytics: To understand which features are used, improve the app experience, and diagnose issues
  • Crashlytics: To detect and fix crashes automatically
  • AdMob: To display non-intrusive banner ads to free-tier users. Pro subscribers see no ads.
  • Gemini AI: When you use AI assistant features (where applicable), your text commands may be sent to Google's Gemini API for processing. No conversation history is stored on our servers.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your data with any third party, except:

  • Google (Firebase, AdMob, Gemini): As described above, for analytics, crash reporting, advertising, and AI processing. Google's privacy policies apply to their services.
  • Google Play Billing: Subscription purchases are processed by Google Play. We do not have access to your payment information.

5. Data Storage & Security

  • All local data is stored in Android's app-private internal storage, encrypted at rest by the OS
  • Network communication (e.g., TV pairing) uses TLS encryption
  • Security certificates are stored securely using Android Keystore-backed encryption
  • All network communication with Google services is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS)
  • Tampered Applications: Our privacy guarantees are strictly void if the application has been modified, tampered with, or installed from an unknown source (e.g., sideloaded APK). We are not liable for data theft or malicious activity conducted by unauthorized versions, even if they spoof or steal our official application signature.

6. Data Retention

  • Local data is retained until you uninstall the app or clear app data
  • Firebase Analytics data is retained for 14 months (Google default)
  • Crashlytics data is retained for 90 days

7. Your Rights

  • Delete your data: Clear app data or uninstall the app to remove all local data. For Firebase data, contact us at the email below.
  • Opt out of ads: Upgrade to a Pro subscription to remove all advertising
  • Opt out of analytics: Disable "Usage & diagnostics" in your Android device settings

8. Children's Privacy

Our applications are rated for all ages and do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. Our apps do not require account creation or personal information to function.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
